# Christos Zervoudakis
**Earth Observation | Remote Sensing | Geospatial Analysis**
 
I'm an Earth Observation analyst with a background in Geology and Geoinformation in Environmental Management. My work focuses on satellite image analysis, geospatial modelling and Python-based processing workflows. I have worked primarily with multispectral imagery, while also gaining experience with SAR data through research activities and specialized training.
 
### Currently
MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Visual Computing, with a thesis on deep learning classification of InSAR time series.
 
### Tools & Technologies
- **Programming:** Python (NumPy, Pandas, GeoPandas, Scikit-learn, PyTorch), MATLAB
- **Geospatial:** QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Google Earth Engine, PostGIS
- **Remote sensing:** SNAP, ENVI, ERDAS Imagine, eCognition
